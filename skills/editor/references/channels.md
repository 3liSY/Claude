# Channel profiles

Ask which channel the video is for if the project name doesn't say. `plan.py init --channel srb-gamer` or `--channel syrian-rubik`.

## SRB Gamer (main channel)
- Gameplay recorded with OBS, no face cam, many different games.
- Voice: mostly Syrian Arabic, English game words. Add the game's terms to `brain/terms.md` first.
- Recipes: `references/editing-rules.md` §11. Start from `analysis/moments.md`.
- Humor allowed: shake zooms, freezes, meme-style hits (licensed only). Budget 1 comic edit per 45–90 s.
- Pace target: about 6–10 edits per minute (zooms, pop-ups, freezes, sounds together).
- Typical long-form. Length varies per video.

## Syrian Rubik
- Hands and cubes, Syrian Arabic voice-over, long-form.
- Recipes: `references/editing-rules.md` §10.
- Never cover hands during an algorithm. Use notation pop-ups at a corner.

## For both
- No music, ever.
- Pop-up style: big, bold, simple, soft shadow, readable on a phone.
- Things he hates: too many effects at once, covering the action (hands, cube, HUD), SFX louder than his voice, the same effect again and again. The validator warns about the busy and repeated cases; you check the rest on the sheets.
- Unsure about an edit? Set `"status": "unsure"` (red marker, not placed) or ask him. Never place a guess silently.
- After a plan he wants the full sheet in the browser and a short list in chat.
